Risland Holdings is a Hong Kong-based multinational real estate company which offers a wide range of services such as residential development, commercial real estate operation, property management and infrastructure construction & operation. By 2018, it has developed projects in many countries including United States, New Zealand, Thailand, India, Indonesia, etc., becoming an innovative driver of urban development and residential environment improvement.

In Indonesia, we are committed to combining Risland’s leading design concepts with the needs of local customers and proud to offer the topnotch apartment product line “Sky House”, which has been on the Indonesian market for 2 years. Sky House Alam Sutera+ is the second project of Sky House in Indonesia.
